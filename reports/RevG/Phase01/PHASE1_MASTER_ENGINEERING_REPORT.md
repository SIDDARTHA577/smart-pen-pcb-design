## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 1 of the **Smart Pen Revision G** engineering program has been executed in full compliance with the Master Engineering Instructions. This audit established a verifiable, evidence-based baseline across all electrical, PCB layout, mechanical CAD, firmware, testing, and manufacturing domains of the project.

### Core Audit Discoveries
1. **Lethal Routing Defects in Client Delivery Archive**: The Main PCB layout in `output/RevF_Client_Delivery/` contains **1,338 DRC violations, 199 track crossings, and 199 dead shorts**, including direct shorts between 3.3V and GND, USB 5V and LiPo battery, and LiPo battery and GND. It is an unmanufacturable, destructive artifact generated by an unconstrained router script. It has been condemned and quarantined.
2. **Authoritative Layout Baseline Identified**: The uncorrupted working PCB layout `output/RevF_Working/SmartPen.kicad_pcb` (185 violations, 33 localized via-on-track conflicts, 29 unrouted ratlines) is designated as the authoritative layout baseline for Revision G.
3. **Catastrophic LDO Regulator Pinout Inversion**: The 3.3V LDO regulator is misnamed `J_PWR` with a 3-pin schematic symbol mapped onto a 5-pin SOT-23-5 footprint. System rail `3V3` is wired to Pin 3 (the Enable pin), while actual regulator output Pin 5 is left floating! The board as designed outputs 0V on the 3.3V rail.
4. **Dangerous Battery Charge Current**: Charge programming resistor R9 (3.3 kΩ) programs 303 mA into the LiPo cell. For a 130–150 mAh cell (mandated by the 15–16 mm barrel constraint), this represents an unsafe 2.0C–2.33C charge rate (max safe limit is 0.5C–1.0C).
5. **Fabricated Power-Path Circuit**: Historical reports claimed dynamic power-path switching using a P-FET (`DMG2305UX`) and Schottky diode (`MBR0520L`). Physical inspection proves these parts do not exist in schematic or BOM. `D3` is an inductive flyback diode for the vibration motor.
6. **Complete Absence of Firmware & Web Application**: Despite detailed handoff specifications, **zero firmware files and zero browser application files** exist in the repository.
7. **100% False-Pass Automated Test Suite**: All 46 pytest tests pass in 0.52 seconds because they rely on hardcoded floating-point multiplications and inspect legacy Revision E files.
8. **Enclosure Envelope Compliance**: The mechanical model strictly complies with **R1** (length 153.50 mm, OD 15.50 mm), **R2** (detachable front MPU on dedicated carrier), and **R3** (battery cradle positioned beneath the electronics rail). However, true 3D solid BRep collision analysis is **NOT VERIFIED** due to missing CadQuery/OpenCASCADE Python modules.

## 5. Summary of Electrical & Power Architecture Findings

1. **LDO Inversion (`J_PWR` / AP2112K-3.3)**:
   - Physical SOT-23-5 footprint Pad 3 is Enable; Pad 5 is VOUT.
   - Schematic routes `/3V3` to Pad 3, leaving Pad 5 floating. System rail 3V3 will never receive power (**0V output**).
2. **LiPo Charging Hazard**:
   - Resistor `R9` = 3.3 kΩ programs $I_{CHG} = 303\text{ mA}$.
   - For a 130–150 mAh cell, charge rate is 2.0C–2.33C. Risk of cell swelling and thermal runaway. Requires recalculation to 10.0 kΩ (100 mA).
3. **USB Connector Parity**:
   - Mid-mount Type-C receptacle `J_USB` pads map VBUS to GND/Data due to pin assignment shift.
4. **Missing Power-Path Switch**:
   - `DMG2305UX` and `MBR0520L` are completely absent from the schematic and BOM.
   - The pen cannot run from USB when switch SW3 is OFF, and cannot boot on a deeply depleted battery.
   - System load draws current in parallel with battery during charging, interfering with MCP73831 C/10 termination.
5. **J_BAT Polarity**:
   - Footprint lacks silkscreen `+` and `-` markings, creating assembly risk.
