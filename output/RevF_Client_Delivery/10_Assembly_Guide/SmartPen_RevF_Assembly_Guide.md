# Smart Pen Revision F — Assembly & Disassembly Guide

Please refer to [ASSEMBLY_INSTRUCTIONS.md](file:///output/RevF_Client_Delivery/10_Assembly_Guide/ASSEMBLY_INSTRUCTIONS.md) for the complete, step-by-step mechanical and electrical assembly and disassembly instructions.

### Summary of Key Assembly Milestones:
1. **Internal Rail Cartridge**: Integrates Main PCB (`84.0 x 9.2 mm`), LiPo battery (`LP401040`, 300 mAh), MicroSD carrier (`11.4 x 16.0 mm`), and vibration motor.
2. **Refill & Push-Rod**: Standard D1 refill ($\varnothing 2.35 \times 67.0\text{ mm}$) coupled to a $\varnothing 1.60\text{ mm}$ Delrin push-rod through the $\varnothing 3.20\text{ mm}$ central tunnel. Minimum axial gap to battery is $5.50\text{ mm}$ (retracted) and $10.00\text{ mm}$ (extended), completely eliminating mechanical interference.
3. **Bayonet Twist-Lock**: 3 radial lugs engage with $30.0^\circ$ clockwise rotation to lock, and $30.0^\circ$ counter-clockwise to unlock for tool-less field extraction.
4. **Dimensions**: Total length $153.50\text{ mm}$ (within 150.0–155.0 mm spec), outer diameter $15.50\text{ mm}$ ($\le 16.00\text{ mm}$ spec).
